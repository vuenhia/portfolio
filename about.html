Why I want to be a programmer:

Since I was young, I've always found joy in videogames. I've always been told that videogames weren't gonna get me anywhere, however, I've never stopped playing them. My favorite parts about games were the learning curves. The harder the game, the more stubborn I was to try and get better at it. My love for games eventually lead to me to wanting to learn how to create games, thus starting my journey to becoming a programmer. It wasn't just games, it was mySpace, Facebook, and many more other websites I've browsed when I was a kid. Coding in a way, has the same charm as videogames for me. The harder it is, the more stubborn I am to try and figure it out. I love the feeling of accomplishment when I finally figure out a problem I've been stuck on for. Through my time at CVTC, I've learned valuable skills in debugging, problem-solving, time management, critical thinking, creativity, and many more.
